About Us - Cafe Jadoel

Welcome to Cafe Jadoel, where we serve traditional Indonesian cuisine with a modern twist. Our mission is to bring the authentic flavors of Indonesia to your table while providing a cozy and nostalgic atmosphere.

Founded in 2020, Cafe Jadoel has quickly become a favorite spot for locals and tourists alike. Our chefs use only the freshest ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations to create dishes that are both delicious and memorable.

We believe in sustainability and supporting local farmers, which is why we source our ingredients from nearby markets. At Cafe Jadoel, we are committed to providing excellent service and a warm dining experience for all our guests.

Thank you for choosing Cafe Jadoel. We look forward to serving you!
